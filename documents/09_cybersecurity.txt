Cybersecurity protects computers, networks, applications, and information from unauthorized access and attacks. Common security practices include authentication, encryption, access control, secure software development, monitoring, and regular updates.
f you mean “give me all the details/topics of Cybersecurity”, here is a complete roadmap from beginner → advanced, useful for college, internships, placements, and GATE-level understanding.
